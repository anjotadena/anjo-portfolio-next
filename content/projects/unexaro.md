## Overview

Unexaro is a modular business platform that Anjo Tadena is building. It is planned to cover four areas of everyday business operations: HR, payroll, point of sale (POS), and inventory. Unexaro is in development — it is not a released product, and there are no public users, customers, or results to report yet.

## Problem

Many businesses run their daily operations across separate tools and spreadsheets: one place for employee records, another for payroll, another for sales, and another for stock. When records are fragmented like this, the same information is typed in more than once, records drift out of sync, and routine work takes longer than it should.

## Planned capabilities

The goal is one connected system in which each area is a module that shares the same underlying records:

- HR — employee records kept in one place.
- Payroll — pay processing that works from the same employee records instead of a separate copy.
- Point of sale (POS) — recording sales.
- Inventory — tracking stock alongside sales.

These are planned capabilities of a product in development. Specific features, the technology stack, availability, and release dates have not been published.

## My Role

Anjo is the creator and developer of Unexaro. He is designing and building the platform.

Unexaro is in development. There is no public demo, repository, or launch date yet. For questions about Unexaro, contact Anjo directly by email.
